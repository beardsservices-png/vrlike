Bedrock Command Guide

The commands that matter for building, written for Minecraft Bedrock on PS5. Each one shows how to type it and examples you can copy. <angle brackets> are required, [square brackets] are optional.

Before you start

Turn on cheatsWorld settings → Cheats → Activate Cheats. That world stops earning trophies; your other worlds aren't affected.
Open chat on PS5Press right on the D-pad, type /, and suggestions pop up as you type. A USB keyboard plugged into the PS5 is much faster.
See your coordinatesSettings → Game → Show Coordinates, or /gamerule showcoordinates true. X is east–west, Y is up, Z is north–south.
Exact spots100 64 -20 means X 100, Y 64, Z −20 in the world.
Relative to you: ~~ ~-1 ~ is the block under your feet. ~5 ~ ~ is 5 blocks east of you.
Where you're looking: ^^ ^ ^5 is 5 blocks straight ahead of where you face, ^-2 ^ ^ is 2 to your left.
Who it affects@s you · @p nearest player · @a all players · @r random player · @e every entity (mobs, items, you).
Narrow it down@e[type=cow] only cows · @e[type=item,r=20] dropped items within 20 blocks · @a[name=Brian].
Block namesLowercase with underscores: black_concrete, smooth_stone. If one is rejected, type the first letters and pick it from the list.
